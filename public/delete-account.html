Delete Your MyDuit Account

MyDuit · https://myduit001.web.app · This page is the web resource for requesting deletion of your MyDuit account and all associated data.

You can delete your MyDuit account and all of your financial data in two ways. Deleting your account is permanent: your transactions, accounts, budgets, debts, and payment records are removed and cannot be restored.

Option A — Delete inside the app (fastest)

If you can still open MyDuit, this deletes everything immediately:

  1. Open MyDuit and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Backup & Restore (or Cadangkan & Pulihkan).
  3. Open the Delete All Data tab.
  4. Check "Also permanently delete my Google login account from MyDuit" and confirm the deletion phrase.
Open MyDuit App

Option B — Request deletion from the web

If you no longer have the app installed, use this page to start a deletion request. Clicking the button below opens a pre-filled request on our public support page (GitHub Issues) — you only need a free GitHub account to submit it.

Do not write your account email address in the request — the developer will reply to the request so you can confirm which Google account to delete. No GitHub account? Reinstall the app and use Option A above — it is the fastest way.

What gets deleted

  • Your MyDuit account — the Google sign-in linked to MyDuit.
  • All financial records — transactions, accounts and balances, categories, monthly budgets, debts, and debt payments.
  • Preferences — base currency and saved settings tied to the account.

After your request is verified, the account and its associated data are deleted. Data kept on your own device (offline cache) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
