Privacy Policy — MyDuit

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 · App URL: https://myduit001.web.app

MyDuit is an offline-first personal finance application designed to help users record income, expenses, account balances, monthly budgets, debts, and PDF financial reports. We collect only the minimum data strictly required to authenticate your account and synchronize your financial records across your devices.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Authentication (Firebase Authentication): When you sign in with Google, we receive your basic profile identifier (uid), display name, email address, and profile photo URL strictly to identify and isolate your private ledger.
  • Financial Records (Cloud Firestore): Transactions, accounts, custom categories, budgets, debt records, base currency preference, and historical FX snapshots that you choose to enter are stored under your private user path (users/{uid}/...) protected by Firebase Security Rules.
  • Local Device Preferences: UI language, selected display currency lens, and filter presets are saved locally in your browser/app storage (localStorage and IndexedDB for offline sync).
  • Camera / Receipt Images (Optional): Only used when you explicitly open the receipt scanner. By default, optical character recognition (OCR) runs locally on your device via Tesseract.js and receipt images are not uploaded to our servers. If you configure your own Gemini API key or external OCR option, the receipt image is sent directly from your device to that provider according to their policy.

2. Third-Party Services Used

  • Google Firebase (Auth & Cloud Firestore): Authentication and encrypted cloud synchronization.
  • Public Exchange Rate Feeds (open.er-api.com / api.frankfurter.dev): Fetches anonymous daily currency conversion rates with zero user identifiers transmitted.

We do not run advertising SDKs, sell personal data, or share financial records with third-party data brokers.

3. Data Security

All data in transit uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. Firestore access is restricted strictly to the authenticated owner (request.auth.uid == uid) with schema validation rules.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You can delete all of your financial data and permanently delete your MyDuit account directly inside the app at any time:

  1. Open MyDuit and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Backup & Restore (or Cadangkan & Pulihkan) in the navigation/header.
  3. Open the Delete All Data tab.
  4. Check "Also permanently delete my Google login account from MyDuit" (optional if you want full account removal) and confirm the deletion phrase.

If you no longer have access to the app and need data removal assistance, contact the developer via the repository contact page: github.com/DavidTimoteus/MyDuit.

5. Ringkasan Bahasa Indonesia

MyDuit hanya menyimpan data login Google (nama, email, UID) serta catatan keuangan yang Anda buat sendiri di ruang Cloud Firestore pribadi Anda. Pemindaian struk bawaan diproses langsung di perangkat Anda tanpa mengunggah foto struk ke server kami. Anda dapat mengekspor seluruh data ke JSON maupun menghapus total seluruh data dan akun kapan saja melalui menu Cadangkan & Pulihkan → Hapus Total.
